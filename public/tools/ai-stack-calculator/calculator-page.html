Frequently asked questions

What counts as an "overlap" in my AI stack?
An overlap is when two or more tools in your stack perform the same core function. The most common examples: paying for both Claude API and ChatGPT Plus (both are AI language models), or running Cursor and GitHub Copilot simultaneously (both are AI coding assistants). When you have overlaps, you're paying twice for the same capability.
How accurate is the cost calculator?
The calculator uses current published pricing for each tool's most common paid plan. Actual costs may vary if you're on a custom enterprise plan or heavy API usage tier. The overlap waste estimate is conservative — real waste is often higher once you factor in time spent context-switching between redundant tools.
What is the Stack Health Score?
The health score is a 0–100 rating of your stack's efficiency. It starts at 95 and deducts points for overlapping tools, stack bloat (too many tools), and high spend relative to tool count. A score above 75 means your stack is well-optimized. Below 50 means you have significant waste to address.
My tool isn't listed — what should I do?
Use the "Other (unlisted)" option and enter your monthly cost manually. We're continuously adding new tools. If you want a specific tool added, join the waitlist and let us know.
Is this calculator really free?
Yes, completely free with no signup required. Hektiq's full dashboard (with persistent tracking, AI recommendations, and benchmarks) is a paid product, but the calculator will always be free.
